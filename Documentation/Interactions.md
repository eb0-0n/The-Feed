# The five interactions

These clips show the current opening in Unity using desktop XR controller simulation.

1. [Start screen](videos/01_Wake_And_Begin.mp4) - Begin the morning and enter Maya's room.
2. [Eva's message](videos/02_Evas_Message.mp4) - Read her messages and send a reply asking her to grab a table.
3. [Last-minute submission](videos/03_Last_Minute_Submission.mp4) - Submit Maya's assignment and see the confirmation on her laptop.
4. [Window](videos/04_Courtyard_Window.mp4) - Open it for some fresh air and hear more of the courtyard outside.
5. [Ending and restart](videos/05_Leave_And_Return.mp4) - Leave once the tasks are done, then return to the beginning.
